Two ways to play

Same cube, two puzzles. Both use an 8×8 board.

Candy match-3

Match Cube

Swap neighboring cubes and clear lines of the same color. You have a short run of moves to reach the score.

  • 8×8 board
  • 25 moves
  • Goal 2,500

How to play

  1. Swap two neighboring cubes.
  2. Lines of three or more of the same color clear.
  3. You have 25 moves. The goal is 2,500 points.

Block placement

Cube Filling

Drag polyomino pieces onto the grid. A row or column that fills from end to end clears, and you get that space back.

  • 8×8 grid
  • Polyomino pieces
  • Clear rows and columns

How to play

  1. Drag polyomino pieces onto the 8×8 grid.
  2. Fill every cell of a row or a column.
  3. A full row or column clears.

On your device

No account

GamexCube plays offline. Scores and other game data stay on the device. There is no sign-up.

Ads

The free app shows ads through Google AdMob. Personalized ads are used where consent allows.
