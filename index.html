Candy match

Match Cube

Swap neighboring cubes and clear lines of the same color.

  • Swap cubes
  • Match colors
  • Clear lines

How to play

  1. Swap two neighboring cubes.
  2. Lines of three or more of the same color clear.
  3. Your best score is stored on the device.

Block placement

Cube Filling

Drag polyomino pieces onto the grid. A row or column that fills from end to end clears, and you get that space back.

  • Polyomino pieces
  • Fill the grid
  • Clear rows and columns

How to play

  1. Drag polyomino pieces onto the grid.
  2. Fill every cell of a row or a column.
  3. A full row or column clears. Your best score stays on the device.
