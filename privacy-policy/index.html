Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

This policy explains how Bicodev Games (developer: Bicodev) handles information for the Android game Block Buster Games (package com.blockbustergames.block_buster_games) and for this website, games.bicodev.com.

Block Buster Games is an offline puzzle collection. It includes Match Cube, Cube Filling, and a Game Hub of ten mini-games: Tap Speed, Simon, Reaction, Tic-Tac-Toe, Higher/Lower, Whack-a-Mole, Color Trick, Math Sprint, Memory Match, and Odd Color. It does not ask you to create an account. Best scores are stored only on the device. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect an advertising ID, approximate location based on IP address, and diagnostics. The same text is published at https://games.bicodev.com/privacy-policy/ and https://games.bicodev.com/privacy-policy.html.

Who we are

Bicodev Games publishes Block Buster Games. Bicodev is the developer. For privacy questions, email info.iosnapk@gmail.com. That is the only contact address for this app and this site.

Information stored on your device

There is no account and no sign-up. Best scores and other game data are stored only on your device. We do not upload your score list to a Bicodev server, because we do not run one for player profiles.

Uninstalling Block Buster Games, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes that local game data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads are served by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • Approximate location based on IP address
  • Diagnostics
  • App interactions used for ads and measurement

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, including personalized ads where consent allows, and to limit invalid traffic. We do not control Google’s ad systems.

Where the law requires a choice, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent message before personalized ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK applies the choice you make. If you do not agree to personalized ads, Google can still show ads that are not based on your advertising profile. The choice is stored on your device.

You can reset or opt out of use of the advertising ID in Android settings. Menu names vary by Android version and phone maker:

  • Android 12 and later: open Settings, then Privacy, then Ads (on some phones: Settings, then Security and privacy, then Ads, or Settings, then Google, then Ads). From there you can delete your advertising ID. After it is deleted, apps receive a string of zeros instead of an identifier.
  • Earlier Android versions: open Settings, then Google, then Ads. You can turn on Opt out of Ads Personalization and use Reset advertising ID if you want a new identifier.

Opting out or deleting the advertising ID limits personalization. It does not turn ads off. Clearing the app’s storage, or reinstalling the app, also clears the consent choice stored on the device. Clearing storage deletes local best scores as well.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

More about the Google Mobile Ads SDK: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

We use Firebase Crashlytics to learn when the app crashes so we can fix bugs. Crashlytics may process:

  • Crash logs
  • Device state at the time of a crash
  • Installation IDs, which Firebase uses to count affected devices

We use this information to diagnose and fix stability problems. It is sent to Google Firebase, which processes it for us as a service provider. Crashlytics is not an account system, and it is not how scores are saved.

Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used so we can improve it. Analytics may process:

  • Usage events
  • An app instance ID

We use these events to see which puzzles and mini-games people play and where the game can be clearer or more reliable. The game has no sign-up, and we do not use analytics to identify you by name.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Show and measure ads through Google
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Understand how the game is played and improve it
  • Reply to email you send us

Google may process advertising, crash, and analytics information on servers in the United States and in other countries where Google operates.

We do not sell personal information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with data brokers. Advertising, crash reporting, and analytics are handled by Google, as described in this policy, when you use the app.

Security

Data sent between the app and Google for ads, crash reports, and analytics is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise absolute security. Best scores stay on your device and are not sent to a Bicodev account server.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Best scores and other game data stored by the app remain on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall Block Buster Games. That is how you delete local game data.

Crash reports, analytics, and advertising records are kept by Google under Google’s retention for those products. We use crash and analytics data to fix bugs and improve the game, not to build a player account.

You can also:

  • Delete or reset your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalization, using the Android settings described above
  • Use the Google UMP consent message, where it is shown, to refuse personalized ads
  • Email info.iosnapk@gmail.com with any privacy request, including a request to access or delete information

Because Block Buster Games has no accounts, we usually cannot look up a person by name in an advertising or analytics system. If you write to us, tell us what you are asking and, if you have them, any identifiers Google’s tools show you (for example an advertising ID you are deleting). We will reply and help with what we can delete or with the Google controls that apply.

Children

Block Buster Games is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use the app. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, email info.iosnapk@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

This website

The pages on games.bicodev.com are static files. We do not add our own analytics or advertising scripts to them, and we do not ask you to sign in here. If you email us, we use your message and your email address to respond and to handle your request. We keep that correspondence only as long as we need it for that purpose.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the last updated date at the top. Please check the date when you visit. If you keep using Block Buster Games after the last updated date changes, the updated policy applies to use after that date.
